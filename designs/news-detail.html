Begin with the real question

A shipment enquiry is a message form, not a live tracking surface. The page should say that plainly before it asks for a reference field or a description.

That distinction makes the experience easier to understand. It also gives an editor a clear content boundary when configuring the form.

Keep the form language useful

Contact details, an enquiry category, a reference field, a message, and consent are enough for this compact demonstration.
